## Case Study 1 — MIXT: From Bottlenecks to Breakthroughs

**Customer:** MIXT — American fast-casual chain (salads, warm bowls, sandwiches; seasonal, locally-sourced), founded 2006
**Scale:** 18 locations
**Delivery channels:** DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, ezCater
**ERP:** Restaurant365

**Challenge:** The finance team struggled with manual third-party reconciliation across multiple systems. Monthly close stretched over weeks, consuming resources on tracking discrepancies between marketplace reports, POS systems, and Restaurant365 entries. Limited expense-tracking granularity reduced visibility into 3P operational costs. The team was also vulnerable to costly chargebacks that hurt profitability.

**Loop solution / features used:**
- Automated journal entry generation and posting
- Proactive reconciliation intelligence
- ERP integration (direct posting to Restaurant365)
- Chargeback dispute support via data surfacing

**Quantified outcomes:**
- 100+ automated journal entries posted monthly
- Month-end close 3x faster
- High-volume 3P sales reconciled across 18 locations and multiple channels
- Eliminated hours of manual tracking, validating, and posting

**Customer quote:** "The automated journal entries have rampantly helped accelerate our month-end close over the course of the past 9 months." — Vincent Laurel, Controller, MIXT

## Case Study 2 — SDC Restaurants: Transforming 3P Financial Management

**Customer:** SDC Restaurants, LLC — multi-location franchisee
**Scale:** 52 Carl's Jr. locations (reporting notes 3 major delivery platforms across 66 locations)
**Executive:** Cody Wong, CFO
**ERP:** Restaurant365

**Challenge:** Each online ordering channel produced complex reports that failed to align with internal data or their ERP (Restaurant365). The finance team was trapped in manual, error-prone reconciliation, creating bottlenecks that delayed operations reporting and prevented strategic financial analysis.

**Loop solution / features used:**
- Loop's **Balance** module — automated third-party marketplace reconciliation, integrated with Restaurant365
- Automated journal entry creation and posting (eliminating manual spreadsheet work)
- Direct connection of third-party marketplaces to core financial systems

**Quantified outcomes:**
- $1M+ in sales reconciled monthly across delivery platforms
- 790+ journal entries automated monthly (replacing manual uploads)
- 200+ hours saved monthly for the finance team
- 20–30 minutes reduced per journal entry cycle
- Cleaner, timely financials enabling on-time operations reporting

**Customer quote:** "Loop AI has made managing 3rd-party financial management significantly easier and reduced the heavy lifting in our team. We fully recommend Loop AI to any restaurant running multiple locations to manage their 3rd-party operations."

## Case Study 3 — Centennial Hospitality Group: Optimizing Delivery Margins at Wing Shack

**Customer:** Centennial Hospitality Group (CHG) — multi-brand restaurant group
**Scale:** 19 locations, 3 brands (Wing Shack, Luna's Tacos & Tequila, Sexy Sammies)
**Delivery channels:** DoorDash, Uber Eats, and other third-party platforms

**Challenge:** Margin erosion despite delivery-driven sales growth — "delivery was growing, but profits weren't keeping up." Pain points: unclear marketing ROI (could not tell if spend drove incremental sales), unpredictable payout ratios affecting forecasting, and lack of cross-platform data integrity for strategic decisions.

**Loop solution / features used:**
- Loop's **TruROI+** capability
- Comprehensive cross-channel data visibility (unified reporting across DoorDash, Uber Eats, and other channels)
- AI-driven ROI analytics and marketing spend optimization (identifying where marketing dollars worked vs. wasted spend)

**Quantified outcomes:**
- +3.0% increase in delivery gross payout
- 30% reduction in marketplace fees
- +8% increase in sales in the first month post-implementation
- Improvements sustained across multiple quarters

**Customer quote:** "Loop was such a welcome addition because it let us compartmentalize and say, 'Cool, we have an advocate who knows third party better.'" — Doug Baker, Technology and Marketing Specialist, CHG
